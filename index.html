      How to play

      The aim

      Be first to 30 points. Half time is called when either side reaches 15. Shorter and longer matches change both numbers. The match ends the moment a side reaches the full-time score, even before a conversion.

      Attacking

      Your team is five dice. The Scrum Half always rolls first, after the kick-off, after every tackle and whenever you win the ball. Most of its faces pass to a named player, who rolls their own die next.

      A ‹Pass› face lets you choose any of the Forward, Centre, Wing or Back. A run shows an intensity number: the higher it is, the harder it is to stop. Each player has one big move: Forward Line Break 6, Centre Thru the Gap 7, Wing Into the Corner 8, Back High Ball 6.

      Tackling

      The defence rolls the brown die twice and adds the numbers. Reach the intensity and the tackle is made; play goes back to the Scrum Half. Fall short and it's a line break: the attacker rolls the black die. TRY scores 5, NO TRY is a changeover (so is RUCK WIN: held up over the line), VIDEO REF means roll again.

      Big Hit on the first brown die against a plain Hit Up of 3 or 4 (not a big move) sets up a ruck contest: the defence rolls the black die and steals the ball on RUCK WIN. Big Hit on the second die always completes the tackle. Two Big Hits injure the ball carrier: that position runs at −1 for the rest of the match.

      Kicking

      Roll the brown die for the range, then the black die's number for the kick. The kick must be at least the range. Each number is 10 metres on the pitch.

      Every try is followed by a conversion. The Centre and Back can try a drop goal: check the range first, then decide whether to kick or pass. A missed drop goal or penalty kick hands the ball over.

      Errors and set pieces

      A Knock On gives the other side a scrum; Into Touch gives them a lineout. They roll the white die: a green light wins the ball, red loses it. A collapsed scrum is reset and rolled again. A lineout that's not straight passes the throw to the other side. Intercepted turns the ball over straight away.

      Penalties and cards

      The white die can award a penalty either way. The black die then checks for a card: the yellow dot sends a player to the sin bin and the offenders take −1 off each brown die they roll in a tackle for the rest of the half. A second yellow makes it −2 a die for the rest of the match.

      The range for the kick is rolled at the same time, so you know the distance before you choose: kick for goal (3 points), or quick tap for +1 intensity on your next 3 runs. The advantage ends after 3 tackles or an error.

      Restarts

      After any score the team that scored receives the kick-off. The coin toss decides who receives first; the other side receives at the start of the second half.
